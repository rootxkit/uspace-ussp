## What to build

- `schemas/alert/v1.json` (every kind of `04 §3.3` with its detail
  object; `state`, `clear_reason`, `policy_version`, `captured_at`) and
  `schemas/traffic/product/v1.json` (`at`, `for` (`intent_id` or `bbox`),
  `tracks[] {track_id, trust, source, age_s, position, alt_amsl_m,
  alt_source, speed_ms, track_deg, vspeed_ms, emergency, identification
  (status only), time_of_report (= ts), peer_unavailable?}`, `alerts[]`
  (active proximity alerts for the subscriber), `degraded[] {input,
  since, reason}`, `cis_version`, `policy_version`), with examples.
- CPA path in `monitor` (same worker loop as WP-10): one
  `alerting.Monitor` per owned cell set with `GridCellM ≥ 800`, fed
  every `track/telemetry/v1`, `peer.v1` and `man.v1` sample of its cells
  and ring-1 as `alerting.Track` (`Flying` from the status; `VNMS/VEMS/VDMS`
  from `rid.VelocityNED`; `AltAMSLM` nullable with `AltSource`; `Backlog`,
  `RxAtS`, `SourceTS`; `Source`/`Station` from `source`/`source_instance`;
  `Transmitter` nil for authenticated and provider tracks; `Identified`
  from the identification block); `Tick` every 1 s; `SwitchSource` on
  `ctl.sources`; `Drop(id, flight_ended)` on flight end. `Events.Raised`
  of kind `conflict` → `alert/v1` `proximity` with the detail names of
  `04 §3.3` (`t_cpa_s`, `d_cpa_h_m`, `d_alt_m`, `peer`), severity from
  the monitor; `Cleared` → `state: cleared` with the reason and the last
  numbers (C-14). Republish `Active()` every tick on `alrt.v1`. Pair
  budget: when the per-tick pair count exceeds `policy.CPAPairBudget`
  (50 000), the worker ticks that cell set at 2 s and sets
  `evaluation_period_s: 2` on its alerts and status; never skips (05 §5).
  Zone and identification kinds from the same monitor are WP-12's to map;
  this WP maps `conflict` and leaves the others on the floor with a
  counter until WP-12 lands.
- `alerts` package (in `api`): consume `alrt.v1` → `alerts` rows (raise
  inserts, update refreshes `detail` and `updated_at`, clear sets
  `cleared_at`, `clear_reason`), idempotent by alert key + `raised_at`;
  `POST /v1/alerts/{id}/ack` (scope `ussp.traffic`, operator must own
  the flight); escalation job: a critical alert unacknowledged for
  `policy.EscalationAfterS` (30 s) is marked `escalated_at` and
  republished to the console stream; delivery record per client
  (`delivery[client_id] = {sent_at, acked_at}`).
- Frame contract (reconciliation M29, M22): every frame on both sockets
  is the common envelope (`schema`, `msg_id`, `producer`, `ts`, `rx_ts`,
  `captured_at`, `time_source`, `backlog`; pinned copy of
  `uspace-lab/schemas/common/envelope/v1`) plus a `body` named by
  `schema`. Server → client: `console/status/v1` on connect and every
  2 s (`connection_id`, `server_ts`, `policy_version`, `stale_after_s`,
  `live_max_age_s`, `dropped_frames`, `degraded[]`, `sources[]`,
  `cis_version`, `cis_age_s`, `evaluation_period_s`, and the alert
  thresholds in force: INV-03, the kit never defaults them);
  `console/snapshot/v1` on connect and after a re-subscribe (`tracks[]`,
  `alerts[]`, `manned[]`, `zones_version`); then `traffic/product/v1`
  and `alert/v1` bodies. Client → server (staff only):
  `console/subscribe/v1 {bbox, layers[]}`. Operators authenticate with
  a bearer (`ussp.traffic`); staff with the `uspace_session` cookie on
  a same-origin upgrade plus an `Origin` allow-list, verified in this
  process with the shared verifier; `4401` = re-login. Machine clients
  and the console parse the same frame.
- `traffic-ws`: `WS /v1/traffic?intent_id=|bbox=` and `WS /v1/alerts?intent_id=`
  (scope `ussp.traffic` for operators: `intent_id` must be theirs;
  staff session for `bbox`, or `console/subscribe/v1`): subscribe to the `cell5`s of the intent's
  envelope (plus `policy.TrafficRadiusM`, 2 km) or the bbox; assemble a
  `traffic/product/v1` at 1 Hz from the latest sample per track within
  radius (own flights, peers, manned, e-conspicuity, with `trust` and
  `age_s` from `captured_at`), active proximity alerts from `alrt.v1`,
  `degraded[]` from `src.v1` and `/readyz`-style input states (manned
  stream absent → `manned: unavailable since T`; DSS down → `dss`; CIS
  stale → `cis`); a track from a disabled source ages out as
  `source_disabled`, a stale one as `stale` with `age_s`, never removed
  silently (B-11, SC-16). Unacknowledged critical alerts repeated every
  `policy.AlertRepeatS` (10 s) on the alerts stream. Server-side
  throttle per subscriber above 200 tracks (2 Hz per track) with a
  `dropped_frames` counter in the product. Every 10 s a sampled product
  is published on `traffic.product.v1.<client_id>` for the record
  (`03 §3` 0.1 Hz). `GET /v1/traffic/snapshot` returns one product.

## Safety notes

- No resolution advice of any kind: the product and the alert carry
  numbers and the peer's identity, never an instruction (X-15, C-11).
  A reviewer greps the schemas and the i18n catalogues for "climb",
  "descend", "hold", "turn".
- Nothing is hidden: a failed input is a `degraded` entry with a time; a
  disabled source is `source_disabled`; a stale track stays with its age
  until the policy's stale time, then clears `stale` (C-18: never because
  of a cap).
- Backlog never raises (T-04): assert it on the CPA path with a backlog
  pair inside the minima.
