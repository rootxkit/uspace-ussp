## What to build

- `coordination/annex_v/v1` is the ANSP's schema (its API carries the
  body; M14): consume the pinned copy from `uspace-lab/schemas/` (until
  then from the ANSP repo at the `SOURCE` commit of `api/clients/ansp.yaml`),
  never redefine it here; our examples under `schemas/examples/consumed/`
  validate against it in CI. Kinds `intent_notice`, `nonconformance`,
  `contingent`, `ended`; for `nonconformance` the `conf.v1` numbers and
  the flight's last position.
- `coordination.Notifier` (in `api`, driven by `conf.v1` and `intent.v1`):
  for an intent whose volumes touch a U-space airspace with
  `in_controlled_airspace` (from `cis_current`) → `intent_notice` on
  activation and `ended` on end; for every `nonconforming`/`contingent`
  transition → the corresponding kind within 5 s; `POST
  {USSP_ANSP_BASE_URL}/v1/coordination/notices` (M2; client generated
  from `api/clients/ansp.yaml`; scope `ansp.coordination`, `aud` = the
  ANSP's host) → `202 {ack_id, state: received, received_at}`; store
  `ats_notified_at` and `ats_ack_ref` on the conformance state; a
  person's acknowledgement (`state: acknowledged`) polled on `GET
  .../notices/{ack_id}` every 10 s for 5 min (no push in v1);
  unacknowledged → `escalated` on the console with age (02 F13),
  retried with backoff when the ANSP is unreachable, counted.
- `records.Flight(ctx, flightID)`: the record of `02 F7`: the intent
  and every version (decision, number, thresholds, conflicts,
  `cis_version_checked`, `registry_checked_at` — no names), the
  telemetry summary (start, end, sample count, gaps as holes with their
  cause from `src.v1` gap records or "no recorded cause", bbox, max
  AMSL), the alerts with their lifecycle, the conformance timeline, the
  traffic products sampled for that flight's operator (what was shown,
  when, degraded flags), `policy_version`s in force; `alerts:
  unavailable` when the alert store cannot be read, never an empty list
  (B-13). Served at `GET /v1/records/flights/{id}` with scope
  `ussp.records`; audited with the caller.
- `records.Daily`: a job at 01:00 UTC builds the previous day's bundle
  (every flight record, JSON lines, gzip) into object storage or a
  local volume (`USSP_RECORDS_DIR`), with SHA-256 in `record_bundles`;
  `GET /v1/records/daily/{date}` streams it with the hash header; a day
  without a bundle after 02:00 → `/readyz` degraded `records: day
  <date> missing` (an alarm on our side; the authority's is its own).
- `records.Sampler`: consumes `traffic.product.v1` → `traffic_products`
  via the TSDB path (if WP-11 left it to the writer directly, this WP
  only verifies the rows exist and are joined in the record).
- Retention jobs (`policy`-driven, defaults of `05 §4`): telemetry 90
  days online (Timescale policy from WP-1, re-read from policy at
  start), `operator_position` nulled after 90 days unless the flight is
  referenced by an occurrence or a flagged incident, peer data 24 h
  (WP-13), alerts/intents/conformance kept 5 years (no job yet; a
  documented decision). Every job logs what it removed and counts.
- `occurrence`: `occurrence.Report` built from a conformance or
  proximity event flagged by a supervisor on the console (`POST
  /v1/admin/occurrences` from WP-18; this WP provides the package and an
  internal endpoint) or automatically for `airprox` when a proximity
  alert's `d_cpa_h_m < policy.AirproxReportM` and `d_alt_m <
  policy.AirproxReportVM` (defaults from the CPA minima), `nonconformance_in_prohibited`,
  `lost_link_in_uspace`, `emergency`; `became_aware_at` = the event's
  time; `deadline_at` = + 72 h; `POST {authority}/v1/occurrences`
  (`occurrences.write`, `aud` = the authority's host, consumed schema
  `occurrence/v1` owned by the authority, client generated from
  `api/clients/authority.yaml`) with retry; `reporter.person_ref` is an
  **opaque reference sent in clear text over TLS** (reconciliation M13:
  the authority encrypts it at rest for its incident officers; there is
  no public-key exchange and nothing here encrypts the field);
  undelivered reports listed for the console with time to deadline; a
  report past its deadline is a `critical` console item.
- `status`: `POST /v1/certificates/{id}/status` to the authority for
  `start` (first time `api` serves with a valid certificate id
  configured and the operator confirms on the console), `cease`,
  `restart`; stored, retried, shown.

## Safety notes

- Records are evidence: a hole is a hole, a missing store says
  "unavailable", a number is the one that was shown at the time
  (`policy_version` and `cis_version` on every item).
- Occurrence data is for safety only (376/2014 Art. 15(2)); the console
  never turns a report into a violation, and nothing here links a report
  to the authority's violations (that table does not exist here).
- No PII in a record or a notice; the emergency contact stays a
  reference.
