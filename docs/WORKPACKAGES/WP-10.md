`internal/conformance/`, `internal/app/monitor` and `cmd/monitor` (the

## What to build

- `conformance.Judge(sample Sample, auth Authorisation, pol Policy)
  Verdict` (pure, stateless, pinned by the vectors): `Sample` = position,
  `alt_amsl_m` (nullable), `alt_source`, `captured_at`, `flying`;
  `Authorisation` = volumes with AMSL bands and time windows (from
  `intent_active`), `deviation_thresholds {h_m, v_m, t_s}`, the Art. 6(1)
  conditions as a list of `Condition` the intent carries (airspace
  operational conditions already applied to the volumes by WP-7, so here
  they reduce to the volume and window); `Verdict` = `inside` or
  `outside` with `reason` (`outside_volume_h`, `above_upper`,
  `below_lower`, `before_start`, `after_end`), `distance_outside_m`
  (horizontal distance to the nearest authorised outline, 0 inside),
  `height_over_m` (above the upper or below the lower AMSL, 0 inside),
  `within_threshold` (outside but within `h_m`/`v_m`: Art. 10(2)(d)
  tolerance), `vertical_known` (false on `alt_source: none`; the vertical
  judgement is then not evaluated and the verdict says so, never
  `inside` by default). A sample with `alt_source: pressure` is judged
  with the pressure margin (`policy.PressureUncertaintyM`) and marked
  `within_band: false` when only the widened test passes (`04 §3.1`).
- `conformance.Tracker` (one per flight, owned by the `monitor` worker
  for the flight's cell): admits samples like `alerting` (backlog
  rejected and counted; late beyond `LiveMaxAgeS` rejected; out of order
  rejected; disabled source rejected), feeds `Judge`, and runs the state
  machine: `conforming` ↔ `nonconforming` when `outside` persists beyond
  `t_s` **or** exceeds the thresholds at once (`threshold_exceeded`),
  with hysteresis `policy.ConformanceClearAfterS` (3 s) back to
  conforming; `nonconforming` → `contingent` after
  `MaxRecoverableTimeInNonconformingStateSeconds` (core constant, 60 s)
  without returning; `lost_link` after `policy.LostLinkS` (15 s) of no
  live sample on an activated intent (`telemetry_lost` at 5 s is WP-8's
  track flag; here it is a reason, not a state); `unknown` when the
  authorisation is missing (`intent_active` has no entry: counted, the
  flight is reported `unknown` on the console, never silently
  conforming). Every transition → `conf.v1.<flight_id>` with the
  verdict's numbers and `policy_version`; every sample → a
  `conformance_samples` row via the TSDB path.
- Alerts (through the `alerts` interface): `nonconformance` (critical)
  raised once on entering `nonconforming`, refreshed every 1 s with
  current `distance_outside_m`/`height_over_m` (C-08), cleared `resolved`
  with its last numbers (C-14) or `flight_ended`/`stale`;
  `lost_link` (critical) raised at `LostLinkS`, cleared on the next live
  sample; `nonconformance_nearby` (warning) to every flight within
  `policy.NonconformanceNearbyRadiusM` of the deviating aircraft's last
  position (neighbours from the same worker's track table), cleared
  when the source alert clears. Severity change = new raise (C-07).
- `monitor` process skeleton: cell ownership from config, JetStream
  consumers on `trk.v1.<cell3>.>` for owned cells plus ring-1 (WP-6
  `cell.Ring1`), the followers for `intent_active`, `policy`,
  `source_control`, `cis_current` (WP-12 uses it), a per-cell worker
  loop with a 1 s tick, `Active()` republish each tick, a status line
  every 10 s (flights tracked, states by count, rejected counters,
  projection ages, `evaluation_period_s`), `/readyz` entries for each
  projection with age. A failing judgement for one flight neither stops
  the tick nor clears what it could not evaluate (C-09).
- Internal `api` endpoint `POST /internal/intents/{id}/state` (loopback
  only, process credential) that WP-7's state machine accepts for
  `nonconforming`/`contingent`/`conforming` so the intent record and
  `intent.v1` follow; WP-13 reads `intent.v1` to write the DSS state.
- `testdata/vectors/conformance.json` (lab shape, ≥ 40 cases): inside;
  outside horizontally by 1 m / by `h_m` − 1 / by `h_m` + 1; above upper
  within and beyond `v_m`; below lower; before start / after end; a
  pressure-altitude sample inside only with the margin; unknown vertical;
  circle and polygon volumes; two volumes in sequence (a corridor);
  stateful sequences: `t_s` persistence then raise, return within
  hysteresis (no clear), return beyond (clear `resolved` with numbers),
  60 s to `contingent`, lost link raise and clear, backlog samples
  ignored, late samples ignored, disabled source clears
  `source_disabled`; invalid thresholds refuse (E-15). Each with `why`.

## Safety notes

- This is the service the regulation names for Art. 13. It alerts
  people; it changes nothing on the aircraft and it sends no advice.
- Separation and conformance are judged in AMSL (D-01). The volumes
  arrive as AMSL from WP-7; this package never converts.
- A sample that cannot be judged (no altitude, no authorisation, backlog,
  late) is counted and visible, never treated as conforming (E-15,
  SC-22). Reading "conforming" on the console must mean the judgement
  ran.
- Thresholds from `policy` with `policy_version` on every state and
  alert; never a literal; never relaxed to pass (INV-03).

## As built

- The internal `POST /internal/intents/{id}/state` was not built: monitor
  and api run in separate containers, so a loopback route cannot reach
  api, and PLAN §3.2 sends hot-path facts to the control plane on
  JetStream with explicit ack. api consumes `conf.v1` instead
  (`conformance.Recorder`, durable `api-conformance`) and moves the
  intent through `intent.Service.SetConformance`; the timeline goes to
  `conformance_states`.
- A lost link is non-conformance: the tracker's base state is
  nonconforming (`telemetry_lost`) from the moment `lost_link` is
  raised, so the intent goes nonconforming and contingent after 60 s
  without the link; the reported state is `lost_link` (`contingent`
  once contingent).
- Contingent is kept until the flight ends (F3548 has no way back to
  Activated); the nonconformance alert itself may still clear
  `resolved` when the aircraft is shown inside.
- `nonconformance_nearby` also fans out from a `lost_link` (from the
  last position); the neighbours come from the process's track table,
  not one worker's, so a flight near a cell boundary is not missed.
- A switched-off source is learnt from `source_control` on the tick
  (the ingest refuses the source's samples, so none arrives).
- `alert/v1` is written with the fields of spec 04 §3.3 until WP-11
  owns the schema file; `conformance/state/v1` is internal and has no
  schema file. Every judged sample is one `CONF` message (tsdb-writer
  records each in `conformance_samples`): at 100 drones at 1 Hz that is
  about 8.6 million messages a day in a stream WP-6 keeps 30 days, a
  retention to revisit (a shorter samples stream) before the 1000-drone
  scale.
- The S-M2 lab SITL scenario is owed (`docs/RUNBOOKS/WP-10.md`); the
  integration done-when ran in process.
