# WP-12 runs: geo-awareness, zone alerts and the standing re-check (S-M1, S-M4)

The brief's lab scenario (INV-02) is two clauses of `uspace-lab`'s
milestones: N-M1 "the USSP raises `restriction_activated` on the
affected intent within one tick" and S-M4 "a lab constraint from the
ANSP triggers `restriction_activated` and an authorisation update", run
against this branch's image with the lab's CISP, ANSP and a SITL
aircraft. It was first made on 2026-10-04 and is recorded at the end
of this file.

What was run is the brief's done-when **in process**, against the real
PostgreSQL + PostGIS, TimescaleDB and NATS JetStream, with the fake CISP
of `internal/testfakes/cisp` (signed publications; restrictions
published as the ANSP's), through the national API with operator tokens
of this USSP's own issuer, and the monitor process started from its
spec. The tests are `test/integration/geo_test.go` (`/v1/geo`),
`test/integration/recheck_test.go` (the re-check) and
`test/integration/zones_test.go` (the zone path); the unit forms are
`internal/geo/tracker_test.go` (SC-03, SC-12 run 2, SC-13 on
uspace-core's monitor), `internal/geo/service_test.go`,
`internal/intent/recheck_test.go` and `internal/app/monitor/zones_test.go`
(the restart under another instance id).
