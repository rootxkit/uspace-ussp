# WP-11 runs: traffic information and CPA proximity alerts (S-M3)

The brief's lab scenario (INV-02) is SC-01 and SC-02 flown in SITL
through the operator WebSocket against this branch's image, with SC-21
attempted and its lateness measured, the raise and clear times and
numbers recorded here. **That lab run has not been made**: the lab does
not yet start this USSP's image with SITL aircraft streaming over the
operator WebSocket (WP-20, lab WP-L*). It is owed before merge
(CLAUDE.md rule 6, safety-relevant review) and is to be recorded below
when the lab runs it.

What was run is the brief's integration done-when **in process**: the
monitor and traffic-ws processes against the real NATS JetStream, api's
alerts record and service in process against the real PostgreSQL +
PostGIS, two operators with an account and a client each, an intent
each in `intent_active`, their serials in `client_bindings`, their
flights in the flights table. Tracks are published on `trk.v1` and
`man.v1` as telemetry-ingest and the manned adapter publish them (a
simulated operator at 1 Hz, not SITL). Every WebSocket frame read is
validated against `envelope/v1` and its schema. The tests are
`test/integration/traffic_test.go`; the unit forms are
`internal/traffic/engine_test.go` (core's monitor driven sample by
sample) and `internal/app/trafficws/trafficws_test.go`.
