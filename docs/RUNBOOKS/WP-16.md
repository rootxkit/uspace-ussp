# WP-16: weather information (Art. 12, optional)

Weather is an optional service (Art. 3(3)) and raises no alert, so the
brief names no lab scenario (INV-02 does not apply: a weather condition
is read by the operator, never an alert, never a rejection). This page
says how to configure it, what the defaults pending GCAA are, how to
check it in a deployment, and what was run.

## Configure

- `USSP_WEATHER_SOURCE=awc:<base URL>` on `api`: the NOAA Aviation
  Weather Center data API format. The api calls
  `<base>/metar?ids=<stations>&format=json` and
  `<base>/taf?ids=<stations>&format=json` every `weather_refresh_s`;
  every call has a 10 s deadline, reads at most 2 MiB and 1000 reports,
  and follows no redirect. A malformed value refuses the start.
- Unset: `GET /v1/weather` answers `503 weather_unavailable` with
  `errors[{field: weather_source, reason: not_configured}]`, every
  decision carries the condition `weather_unavailable` with
  `weather_checked_ref` null, and `/readyz` shows `weather` up with the
  reason as its detail (an optional service left unset, as
  `USSP_RECORDS_DIR`).

## Policy values (defaults pending GCAA)

Art. 12 names no station, area or figure; these are the policy row's
values, versioned with `policy_version`, and their defaults are
**pending GCAA**:

| Value | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `weather_station_ids` | `UGTB`, `UGKO`, `UGSB` | stations asked for; empty answers `no_stations` |
| `weather_area_radius_m` | 20 000 | a product's area (and its QNH's) around the station |
| `weather_refresh_s` | 600 | poll period (from 60 to 86 400) |
| `weather_stale_s` | 1800 | no delivery for longer is `stale` |
| `weather_observation_valid_s` | 3600 | how long a METAR or SPECI is in force |
| `weather_advisory_wind_ms` | 10 | wind or gust at or above it is a `weather_advisory` condition; 0 is off |

Open with GCAA: whether any U-space airspace requires the weather
service, and which source it names as trusted (Art. 12(1)).

## Check a deployment

- `/readyz` `weather`: `up` (delivered within `weather_stale_s`),
  `degraded` with "the weather source has failed since <time>: <why>",
  "last delivered N s ago", "never delivered" or `no_stations`, `down`
  when its state cannot be read.
- `/metrics`, component `weather`: `weather_fetched`,
  `weather_fetch_failed`, `weather_report_refused` (a report that did
  not parse whole or disagreed with its envelope; the reasons are in
  the warning log `weather reports refused`), `weather_product_stored`,
  `weather_product_pruned`, `weather_answer_stale`,
  `weather_store_failed`, `weather_status_not_recorded`; component
  `intent`: `intent_weather_unavailable`, `intent_weather_stale`,
  `intent_weather_advisory`.
- `weather_source_status` holds the last outcome per source on the
  database clock (it survives a restart); `weather_products` one row
  per report.
