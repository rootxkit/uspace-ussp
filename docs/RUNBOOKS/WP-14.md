1. The brief's done-when list in process, against the fakes: the fake
   ANSP's stream (`internal/testfakes/ansp`), a fake peer F3411 Service
   Provider (`internal/testfakes/peersp`), `internal/dss/fakedss`, and a

real tsdb-writer for `peer_flights`.
