# WP-10 runs: conformance monitoring (S-M2)

The brief's lab scenario (INV-02) is the S-M2 clauses "conformance
raises and clears on a SITL aircraft leaving its volume or its
deviation thresholds, including height above the authorised upper;
nearby operators get `nonconformance_nearby`; lost link after the
configured silence", run by `uspace-lab` against this branch's image
with the times recorded here (date, image digest, raise and clear
timestamps, numbers). **That lab run has not been made**: the lab does
not yet start this USSP's image with a SITL aircraft streaming over the
operator WebSocket (WP-20, lab WP-L*). It is owed before merge (CLAUDE.md
rule 6, safety-relevant review) and is to be recorded below when the
lab runs it. The ANSP acknowledgement and the peer `Nonconforming`
clauses are WP-15's and WP-13's.

What was run is the brief's integration done-when **in process**: the
monitor process and api's intent service and conformance recorder
against the real PostgreSQL + PostGIS, TimescaleDB and NATS JetStream,
with the integration suite's fake CISP and authority; the intent is
filed and activated through the national API; tracks are published on
`trk.v1` as telemetry-ingest publishes them (a simulated operator, not
SITL). The tests are `test/integration/conformance_test.go`.
